##### **A report-grounded vision-language foundation model for colonoscopy from 280000 routine reports**
2607.28466v1 by Jia Yu, Yan Zhu, Yili He, Zilong Wang, Xinyang Jiang, Peiyao Fu, Ruijie Yang, Tianyi Chen, Siyuan Li, Zhihua Wang, Fei Wu, Quanlin Li, Xian Yang, Pinghong Zhou, Shuo Wang

Vision-language models remain underused in colonoscopy despite the rich expert descriptions recorded in routine reports. These reports document lesion appearance, size and location but summarise entire procedures rather than caption individual frames, leaving clinical findings only weakly linked to the corresponding images. Here we develop EndoCLIP, a colonoscopy vision-language foundation model trained on 125,756 lesion-level image-text pairs progressively recovered from 280,476 routine colonoscopy records. Across lesion-level image-text retrieval, structured report generation and six multi-centre clinical classification tasks, EndoCLIP outperforms general-purpose and biomedical vision-language encoders in both zero-shot and linear-probe settings. On benign-versus-malignant classification, its linear probe approaches the performance of expert readers in a blinded study involving 12 endoscopists. These results suggest that recovering finding-to-frame correspondence can transform routine documentation into scalable supervision, enabling clinical targets to be specified in language rather than separately annotated for each task.

摘要：視覺-語言模型在結腸鏡檢查中仍然未被充分利用，儘管在常規報告中記錄了豐富的專家描述。這些報告記錄病變的外觀、大小和位置，但總結整個程序，而不是對單個幀進行標註，導致臨床發現與相應圖像之間的聯繫僅僅是微弱的。在這裡，我們開發了 EndoCLIP，一個結腸鏡檢查視覺-語言基礎模型，該模型在 125,756 個病變級別的圖像-文本對上進行訓練，這些對逐步從 280,476 份常規結腸鏡檢查記錄中恢復而來。在病變級別的圖像-文本檢索、結構化報告生成和六個多中心臨床分類任務中，EndoCLIP 在零-shot 和線性探測設置下均超越了通用和生物醫學視覺-語言編碼器。在良性與惡性分類中，其線性探測接近於在涉及 12 名內鏡醫生的盲測研究中專家讀者的表現。這些結果表明，恢復發現與幀之間的對應關係可以將常規文檔轉變為可擴展的監督，從而使臨床目標能夠用語言指定，而不是為每個任務單獨註釋。

##### **Machines that know they are aging: a framework for hardware-aware autonomous intelligence**
2607.28451v1 by Cheng Siong Chin, Jianhua Zhang, Mohan Venkateshkumar

In the clinical setting, state-of-the-art LLM judges often fail to identify problematic reasoning segments, over-accepting fluent but weakly grounded responses. Our results show that QA evaluation should account for how inferential relations compose across a reasoning trace, rather than relying only on final answers or LLMs as verifiers. UroReason will be made available through an API, and our code will be released as open source.

摘要：AI 生成的答案在高風險領域中通常流暢，但難以驗證，尤其是當它們包含多步推理而不是單一最終答案時。我們提出了一種基於推理的無參考框架，用於審計 LLM 生成的輸出。該方法將生成的推理痕跡分解為片段，使用自然語言推理 (NLI) 標記局部前提-目標關係，並將這些關係組織成超圖。然後，確定性向後 AND-OR 搜索分配片段級別的審計標籤，指示每個片段在生成的回應中是如何被基礎化的。我們在兩個設置中評估該框架：使用 Hard2Verify 的演繹數學推理，以及使用 UroReason 的開放式醫學推理，後者是一個新的醫生標註的 LLM 推理痕跡基準，來自真實臨床案例。在這些設置中，我們的 NLI-超圖審計提供了比直接 LLM 作為評判基準更可靠的無參考評估信號。在臨床環境中，最先進的 LLM 評判者經常無法識別有問題的推理片段，過度接受流暢但基礎薄弱的回應。我們的結果顯示，QA 評估應考慮推理痕跡中推理關係的組合，而不僅僅依賴最終答案或 LLM 作為驗證者。UroReason 將通過 API 提供，我們的代碼將作為開源釋出。
